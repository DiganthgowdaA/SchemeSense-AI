SchemeSense - Ai

Every scheme, matched to the right person. Every application, routed to the right agency.

A prototype for a citizen-facing app that listens in the applicant's own language, ranks the government or bank schemes they actually qualify for, and hands the application straight to the nearest capable agency.

Built for Smart India Hackathon, for the problem track AI‑powered scheme matching and smart application routing

Speaks the applicant's language

Onboarding by voice, not forms — for users who don't type easily.

Ranks schemes by real fit

Income, purpose, and project cost checked against every scheme's rules — not a static list.

Routes, doesn't just list

Sends the application to the nearest agency that supports the scheme and has capacity.
